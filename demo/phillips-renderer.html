About Plastic Ratio Sampling
The Plastic Constant ρ ≈ 1.324718 is the unique real solution to x³ = x + 1. Similar to the Golden Ratio but with distinct geometric properties, it provides optimal low-discrepancy sampling to prevent moiré patterns in Gaussian Flat rendering.
